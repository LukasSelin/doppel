Reading this screen

Each step is one revision, analysed on its own. The panel on the right is the transition into the selected revision: what happened to every function, and which near-duplicate pairs those changes created or dissolved.

Every step was measured at one pinned operating point. Doppel's thresholds are normally derived from the corpus, and a series that recalibrated per revision would compare answers to different questions. This one did not — the operating point is in the colophon.

Still, most numbers here are corpus-relative. The learned concept vocabulary, roles, habitat fit and the nearest-neighbour percentiles are all properties of each revision's own corpus. Read the per-step figures as a measurement of that revision, not as one measurement over time.

        Reading this screen

        A track is one function followed across the whole series. It is joined only through one-to-one matches, so at every boundary it crosses the matcher matched exactly one function to exactly one other — a rename or a move continues the same line.

        A split or a merge ends a track. There is no evidence saying which of several parts inherits the lifeline, so nothing here pretends one did; the ending is labelled instead.
